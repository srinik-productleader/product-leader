PRODUCT CASE STUDY · INTELLIGENT AUTOMATION · FINANCE

Accounts Payable Automation

Move accounts payable from invoice-heavy manual processing to an intelligent, touchless and control-driven operating model.

PRODUCT ROADMAP

From foundation to intelligent optimisation.

A progressive product journey that starts with a strong digital foundation, automates repeatable work, introduces intelligent decision support and continuously improves from product outcomes.

01

Capture

  • Ingest invoices from email, portals and documents
  • Classify invoice types and suppliers
  • AI-assisted invoice data extraction
  • Create a trusted invoice data layer
Reliable digital invoice intake
02

Validate

  • PO and non-PO validation
  • Supplier and duplicate checks
  • Tax and data validation
  • Route exceptions for review
Higher accuracy and stronger controls
03

Automate

  • Approval workflow orchestration
  • Automated three-way matching
  • Exception handling and escalation
  • ERP posting and status updates
Increased touchless processing
04

Optimise

  • Payment and cycle-time analytics
  • Supplier performance insights
  • Bottleneck and exception analysis
  • Continuous improvement of automation rules
Lower cost and better visibility

PRODUCT OWNER VIEW

How I would drive the roadmap

Customer first

Prioritise journeys where customer effort, operational pain and business value intersect.

Value before complexity

Start with measurable, high-value use cases and expand as adoption and outcomes prove the case.

Responsible AI

Build governance, security, human oversight and auditability into the product.

Engineering partnership

Validate integrations, data dependencies, feasibility and operational readiness early.

Measure outcomes

Use adoption, cycle time, quality, exceptions, customer effort and business outcomes to guide priorities.

Learn and iterate

Use user feedback and product data to continuously refine journeys, automation and the roadmap.

PRODUCT CASE STUDY · AI · RISK & COMPLIANCE

KYC Automation

Transform KYC from document-heavy manual review into a risk-based, intelligent and continuously monitored operating model.

PRODUCT ROADMAP

From foundation to intelligent optimisation.

A progressive product journey that starts with a strong digital foundation, automates repeatable work, introduces intelligent decision support and continuously improves from product outcomes.

01

Collect

  • Digital KYC data collection
  • Document capture and classification
  • Pre-fill and data quality checks
  • Define customer and regulatory journeys
Complete and consistent KYC data
02

Verify

  • Identity verification
  • Document authenticity checks
  • Data cross-validation
  • Route exceptions for specialist review
Faster and more consistent verification
03

Assess

  • Risk-based screening workflows
  • Rules and AI-assisted review
  • Prioritise cases by risk and complexity
  • Human-in-the-loop decision controls
Efficient risk assessment with stronger controls
04

Monitor

  • Ongoing KYC review triggers
  • Change and exception monitoring
  • Audit-ready case history
  • Analytics to improve review prioritisation
Continuous compliance and better efficiency

PRODUCT OWNER VIEW

How I would drive the roadmap

Customer first

Prioritise journeys where customer effort, operational pain and business value intersect.

Value before complexity

Start with measurable, high-value use cases and expand as adoption and outcomes prove the case.

Responsible AI

Build governance, security, human oversight and auditability into the product.

Engineering partnership

Validate integrations, data dependencies, feasibility and operational readiness early.

Measure outcomes

Use adoption, cycle time, quality, exceptions, customer effort and business outcomes to guide priorities.

Learn and iterate

Use user feedback and product data to continuously refine journeys, automation and the roadmap.

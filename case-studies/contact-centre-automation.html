PRODUCT ROADMAP

From foundation to intelligent orchestration.

A progressive product journey: establish the platform foundation, automate high-volume journeys, augment agents with AI, and move toward proactive intelligent engagement.

01

Foundation

  • Map priority customer journeys and pain points
  • Unify channel and interaction requirements
  • Define KPIs, governance and architecture
  • Establish integration and data foundations
MVP scope and product foundation
02

Automate

  • Conversational self-service for priority intents
  • Automate common workflows and service requests
  • Introduce intelligent routing
  • Measure containment, resolution and effort
Faster resolution and lower manual effort
03

Augment

  • AI agent assistance and knowledge support
  • Interaction summaries and next-best actions
  • Quality and coaching workflows
  • Expand automation to complex journeys
Higher agent productivity and consistency
04

Orchestrate

  • Agentic workflows for multi-step tasks
  • Proactive customer engagement
  • Journey optimisation using analytics
  • Continuous outcome-led prioritisation
Intelligent, proactive customer experience

PRODUCT OWNER VIEW

How I would drive the roadmap

Customer first

Prioritise journeys where customer effort, frustration and business value intersect.

Value before complexity

Start with high-volume, measurable use cases and expand when adoption and outcomes justify investment.

Responsible AI

Build governance, security, human oversight and operational readiness into the product.

Engineering partnership

Validate feasibility, integrations, data dependencies and operational constraints early.

Measure outcomes

Use adoption, containment, resolution time, customer effort, quality and efficiency to guide priorities.

Learn and iterate

Use customer and agent feedback to continuously refine journeys, automation and the roadmap.
